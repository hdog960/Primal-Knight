# Alpha 0.15.2 — Fortitude Breach Ward and Paired Earned Retry Comparisons

**Phase:** First Watch & Progression Balancing, slice 3. **Release gate:** 71 required automated checks, including JS syntax.

## Reason
0.15.1 real-engine seeded first attempts with six permanent Training ranks showed Blade First clearing 6/7/7 waves for 51 Essence, Even Watch 6/6/6 for 52, and Stoneguard 6/5/6 for 59. Offense already prevented breaches and kept scaling into the next wave; Fortitude's +12 maximum wall HP per rank improved wall capacity but did not directly mitigate individual breaches. An explicit small defensive effect offers more meaningful investment choice without granting currencies or reducing enemy difficulty for every build.

## Actual production change
Fortitude Training retains **+12 maximum wall HP per purchased rank**. Its first six ranks now additionally reduce the *incoming damage from each fortress breach by 4%*, cumulative and capped at **24%** at rank six. Ranks seven through the permanent cap continue to add HP, without increasing breach ward further. This modifier multiplies the existing enemy suppression/Rampart/Wardkeeper/Sentinel factors; it does not bypass or weaken other defensive paths. No new skill-point ranks, reward triggers, spend automation, bought gear, enemy health, damage-per-wave rewards or save fields. The character's original `training.fort` rank drives the modifier, including for older existing characters. Rank prices remain unchanged, and both the Power training description and Battle Quartermaster explain the exact mechanic.

## How measured
The new `tests/first-watch-defensive-balance.js` runs the actual production engine with the new ward and an otherwise identical test instance with only the `*(1-fortitudeBreachWard())` multiplier removed (the prior 0.15.1 formula). It buys the original priced six-rank investment plans, starts genuine Tier I sieges at wave 1 with deterministic random seeds, and checks actual combat and save integrity; after real defeats it spends only already-earned Essence for a second and, where needed, third attempt. An initial GitHub check showed that two attempts did not reach the seven-minute second First Watch chapter, so the test now measures genuine chapter reach/shortfall rather than assuming a milestone that was not earned. Matched seed comparisons distinguish formula effects from arbitrary build-fixture advantages; no timers, Sigils, XP, ranks or one-time rewards are fabricated. No-Fortitude controls must remain unchanged. Numeric cap tests confirm that higher Fortitude ranks never create more than 24% ward. The log measures but does not guarantee Stoneguard parity with Blade First.

## Future work
Follow with broader policies and longer earned-progression cohorts, expense breakdowns and mid/late bottleneck audits. Neither a complete 12.5-hour real-player cohort nor the 1,250-hour conquest target has been validated; real iPhone graphics/touch/thermal QA stays pending at user direction.

## Verified seeded outcomes and remaining bottleneck
GitHub's 71-check quality gate passed the matched first-siege comparison and the up-to-three legitimate-earned-attempt cohorts. Under identical initial seeds, **Blade First** was 6/7/7 before and after; **Even Watch** was 6/6/6 before and after; **Stoneguard** rose from 6/5/6 to **7/6/6** genuine clears. After up to three attempts, old-versus-new Stoneguard clear counts were **8/8/8** versus **10/8/9**. This shows a real, bounded improvement, not parity with the offense path.

The second First Watch chapter demands **seven active minutes and eleven unique first-clears**. All six Blade First/Even Watch scripted cohorts earned it by their third attempt; **none of the three Stoneguard cohorts** earned it despite 10.36–12.29 real active minutes, because their best unique wave clears were only **10, 8, and 9**. The absence of that reward is an important diagnostic. No artificial time/unique clear flag or Sigils should be granted; subsequent Alpha 0.15.3 work should focus on more effective, fairly priced recovery choices for defense-oriented players. This does not validate the full 750-minute First Watch or 1,250-hour conquest target.
