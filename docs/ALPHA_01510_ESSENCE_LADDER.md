# Alpha 0.15.10 — Quartermaster Essence Ladder & Chapters IX–X Earned Frontier Study

## Playable change
Once First Watch **Chapter VIII is genuinely claimed**, the actual Battle lobby includes a collapsed **Quartermaster Essence Ladder** until Chapter X completion. The view reads current character Hero Essence and real next-rank prices for four meaningful permanent Training paths: Might (damage), Reflexes (attack interval), Fortitude (wall durability), and Attunement (Hero Essence gain). For each, show the exact next normal rank price, cumulative cost for up to three consecutive ranks, the number of consecutive ranks affordable from the *present* Essence balance, and actual next-rank shortfall. It uses the original `trainCostAt` for the original character rank and never guesses future income, changes prices, buys anything, replaces assets or adds any saved data. The button only opens the existing Training menu. The original charcoal/bronze layout becomes one column on narrow mobile screens, with a minimum 44px touch target.

## Test approach
The 79th mandatory quality check `tests/first-watch-essence-ladder.js` tests synthetic fixture **display math only** (independent price formula, caps, owned balance, no saved mutations and legitimate menu navigation). These synthetic chapter records and time are *not* evidence of earned gameplay. A wholly separate deterministic real-engine cohort then performs two seeded matched routes, each with 28 complete natural sieges: identical Tier I→VI earned baseline from 0.15.9 and identical earned equipment enhancement/Ember Rune selection, then either repeat Tier VI or pursue unlocked Tier VII and subsequent territories. Each route retains actual permanent prices, earned Hero Essence, finite first-clear/Sigil rewards, Chapter VIII achievement, original save readbacks and reload. Checkpoints record current Might rank, real next rank cost, current balance, and actual new-first-clear deficit. If Chapter IX or X was not granted naturally, do not claim it was. All results are scripted deterministic strategies, not measured human play.

## Open limits
This is not a complete 750-active-minute First Watch simulation; it is not proof of typical human progression, the proposed ~1,250-active-hour completion time, full mid/endgame upgrade affordability, or physical iPhone visual, touch or thermal acceptance. The original gates and player saves remain unchanged; iPhone manual acceptance is postponed until the locked Alpha 0.15 development roadmap is complete, as directed.
