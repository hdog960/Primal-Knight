# PRIMAL KNIGHT — Game Vision & Non-negotiables

## Experience

A **single controllable character** in the sense of one persistent hero; combat itself is *automatic*. The camera is placed **behind the red-cloaked hero looking over the fortress wall toward the enemies**, never facing the hero and never controlling a multi-hero squad. The environment is 2.5D: a layered fantasy battlefield with depth, perspective, scenery and animated 32-bit-inspired creatures. This is NOT a tower-placement game. The hero fires automatically; fortress stations are auxiliary upgrades.

## Art standard

The user's highest priority is **polished, beautiful and consistent visual art**. No geometric prototype enemies, icon-shaped placeholder soldiers, or unstyled developer debug screens should be considered acceptable final presentation. The provided reference had bright fantasy mountains, forests, stone battlements and red archers; our camera should be the reverse angle behind ONE archer. Character sprites should have distinct materials, contour detail, lighting, coherent scaling, directional cues, animation frames, and projectile effects. Avoid inconsistent sprite styles. Alpha 0.1 has polished scene-source art but still requires a full environment layering and animation pass.

## Progression promise

- A real XP-driven player level awarding skill points. Four or more **massive**, branching, multi-specialization skill trees.
- Equipment loot across many tiers/rarities, enhancements, refining, an economy, exciting unique modifiers and item identities. Not simply a single linear DPS curve.
- Runes dropped/crafted and combined through many tiers via **3 same runes + in-game Gold**, equipped in gear sockets.
- A long-lived, separate **Hero Training** board with a distinct earned currency and many upgrades extending into hundreds or thousands of levels, and sensible diminishing-return limits where required.
- Defensive stations (ballista, laser, cryo, gold transmutation, stun, gravity, etc.) permanently improved over time. They must each visibly appear on the wall when complete and alter gameplay.
- Research that makes other upgrades cheaper and boosts the economy; separate enemy suppression research that weakens hordes.
- Meaningful combinations/synergies; players should choose between damage, defense, farming, survival, and novel skills.
- Structured stage/campaign and endless wave pushing, escalating enemies, varied boss encounters, and eventual ascension systems.

## Practical constraints

- Only ChatGPT + user + GitHub, no Work/Codex/Vercel or paid third-party services.
- Prefer free static GitHub Pages distribution, playable on iPhone browser, landscape game view, touch-friendly menus.
- Permanent progress stored locally initially, export/import backup, no hosted database or subscription required.
- Do not overpromise the Alpha as the finished game. Art pass and system depth are long-term project commitments.
